FarmDirect Privacy & Data Protection Policy

How we protect farmer land records, transactional escrow records, and consumer data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect verified agricultural information including farmer identity, land records (Patta/Chitta and e-Nilam extracts), crop harvest dates, NABL laboratory pesticide test reports, cold-chain GPS location, and buyer delivery addresses.

2. Escrow & Direct Bank Security

All banking credentials, UPI IDs, and digital escrow accounts are processed via bank-grade encrypted banking systems. Funds are held in escrow trust until physical delivery OTP verification.

3. Temperature Monitoring Transparency

Cold-chain sensor readings (temperature and humidity) are securely recorded on the platform to guarantee food safety compliance.
