Terms of Service & Fair Agri-Trading Standards

Fair pricing, quality assurance standards, and escrow release terms across the FarmDirect platform.

1. Fair Farmgate Pricing Mandate

FarmDirect guarantees that at least 85-90% of consumer purchase value is paid directly to verified farmer and FPO bank accounts with zero middleman commission deductions.

2. Cold-Chain & Quality Compliance

Produce listed as Grade A or Organic must pass multi-spectral computer vision testing and comply with cold-chain transit standards (2°C - 6°C).

3. Instant Escrow Release Mechanism

Upon doorstep receipt and OTP confirmation, the payment escrow automatically releases the net payout to the farmer without administrative delays.
