How Harvests Move From Farmgate to Consumer

Every harvest is authenticated through 5 verified phases ensuring guaranteed farmer payments, zero transit spoilage, and certified crop freshness.

01
Farmgate Onboarding

Land KYC & Harvest Listing

Farmers verify agricultural acreage with Patta/Chitta and 7/12 land records. Produce is listed with real-time AGMARKNET Mandi benchmark pricing guidance.

  • Geo-tagged field boundary verification
  • FPO collective harvest pooling for B2B
  • Automated mandi pricing benchmarks

AI Vision Lab

AI Quality & Brix Inspection

செயற்கை நுண்ணறிவு தரச் சோதனை

Computer-vision ML algorithms scan produce images for ripeness index, optical surface defects, sugar Brix content, and GI authenticity certification.

  • 99.2% accuracy computer vision scoring
  • GI-Tag & pesticide-free badge accreditation
  • Smart QR farm-to-fork batch traceability

03
Fair-Trade Engine

Zero-Middleman Smart Escrow

நேரடி வர்த்தகம் & வங்கி பாதுகாப்பு

Consumers and B2B wholesale buyers purchase directly at transparent farmgate rates. Order funds are locked in RBI-compliant automated escrow banks.

  • 88% revenue directly returned to farmer
  • Zero commission middleman deductions
  • Sovereign AES-256 encrypted trade ledger

04
Cold-Chain Radar

AI Route & 3.8°C IoT Logistics

குளிர்சாதன வழித்தட விநியோகம்

AI Route Optimizer plans multi-farm pickup routes. GPS-tracked temperature-controlled vans maintain strict 3.8°C cooling to eliminate spoilage across highway corridors.

  • Multi-stop route optimization engine
  • Live temperature-controlled monitoring (3.8°C)
  • Smart rural aggregation pre-cooling hubs
Launch Live GPS Radar
05
Instant Payout

OTP e-POD & Instant UPI Settlement

உடனடி வங்கி & UPI பரிவர்த்தனை

Upon doorstep or warehouse receipt, a one-time password (OTP) verifies digital proof of delivery (e-POD). Escrow funds are instantly credited to the farmer's bank account.

  • Contactless digital proof of delivery (e-POD)
  • Instant UPI / IMPS direct escrow release
  • Zero payment hold or debt cycle exploitation
