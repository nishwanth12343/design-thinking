Frequently Asked Questions

How does direct farmer payment work?

When a buyer places an order, the payment is held in an encrypted digital escrow. As soon as the delivery is completed and verified via OTP, 85-90% of the funds are instantly transferred directly to the farmer's registered bank account via UPI.

How is cold-chain temperature guaranteed?

Every vehicle and rural hub is fitted with IoT telematics sensors that log temperature every 60 seconds (maintained at 2°C - 6°C). If any thermal breach occurs, the platform automatically flags the batch for re-inspection.

How do FPOs aggregate crop consignments?

FPO managers can use our FPO Hub to pool smallholder harvests into multi-ton consignments for B2B buyers like supermarket chains, ensuring small farmers receive high-volume bulk pricing.
